Export & Upload to Live Shopify Store

Your complete Lumière Cosmetics theme and product catalog are packaged into standard Shopify OS 2.0 format. You can upload the theme ZIP directly to your live Shopify online store in seconds!

📖 How to Publish Live on Shopify (3 Steps):

  1. Log in to Shopify: Go to your Shopify admin panel (e.g. https://admin.shopify.com/store/your-store-name).
  2. Upload Theme: In the left sidebar, navigate to Online Store → Themes. Under "Theme library", click Add theme → Upload zip file, select lumiere-shopify-theme.zip, and click Publish.
  3. Import Products: Go to Products → Import in the Shopify sidebar, upload shopify-products-import.csv, and click Import Products. All your images, descriptions, prices, and tags are live immediately!
